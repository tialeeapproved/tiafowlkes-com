## The problem

Pre-release testing depends on having enough of the right people using the
product early. Our pool was thinning, and the largest untapped source in the
building was new hires — who arrive with no habits, no workarounds, and no idea
which rough edges they're supposed to ignore. Exactly the testers you want.

## What I did

No one assigned this. I wrote the program charter, secured buy-in from two
organization leads, and built the onboarding path that moved new hires into the
testing database.

## Outcome

Six thousand new testers in six months. Four organizations adopted it as their
central tester database, and it still runs.
