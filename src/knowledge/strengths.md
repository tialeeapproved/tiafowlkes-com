# Strengths

Asked what Tia's strengths are, answer from this file.

**A strength is a competency, not a program or a number.** "3P partnership
architecture" is a thing she delivered; "influence without authority" is a capability
she has, and the LG partnership is one of several proofs of it. Lead with the
competency. Put the evidence underneath it. Never let a program name or a metric be
the headline — that turns a strengths answer into a second résumé.

Name three or four, not all six. Pick the ones the question or the role actually
calls for.

---

## Influence without authority

Moves organizations that do not report to her, and gets other teams to adopt her
standards over their own.

- Aligned three competing program leads — Android TV Setup, Google TV, Chromecast —
  onto a single centralized intake model, ending cross-team competition for the same
  participant pool.
- Drove **100% adoption** of one issue and SLA tracking standard across Fitbit, Home
  and Health, replacing each team's own tooling.
- Secured executive approval for incremental headcount on an understaffed vendor
  migration rather than absorbing the gap silently.
- Ran executive alliance management between Google and LG as two peer enterprises,
  not vendor and client.

## Operating in ambiguity

Builds the first version of something where no blueprint exists, and does it without
waiting for the mandate.

- Authored the initial validation framework for **Matter over Thread** and **Matter
  over Wi-Fi** on hub architecture with no prior model to work from.
- Built the **0-to-1** integration, testing and compliance framework for third-party
  platform expansion — now the reference model for subsequent partners.
- Authorized backward-compatibility validation against **unreleased hardware**, a
  first for the entertainment portfolio, and made it standard practice afterwards.
- Secured budget and ownership for Matter over Thread and Spatial Audio when neither
  had a defined owner, a budget line, or an entry point in the pipeline.

## Judgment under fixed constraints

Treats the commitment as fixed and the plan as liquid. When the ground moves, the
date holds and everything underneath it is rebuilt.

- Held the executive approval gate through a late-stage SKU pivot and a factory RF
  desense issue by swapping the entire test cohort onto different hardware mid-flight
  rather than pausing validation.
- Consolidated ten fragmented releases into one synchronized gate to meet a
  court-ordered deadline — **720K+ devices**, **10M+ in market**, one release window.
- Converted launch readiness reviews from interpretive debate into decisive
  data-driven gating by removing the metric the debate was happening over.

## Institutional leverage

What she builds outlives the program that produced it, and keeps running without her.

- **Six operational systems** still in use across four organizations, spanning
  participant infrastructure, SLA tracking, launch governance, escalation and
  knowledge management.
- Program tracking infrastructure adopted across **Home, Health and Pixel**.
- The telemetry integration model she established is now used across the entire
  hardware vertical.
- The control-tower structure built for a court deadline became the template
  executive leadership reused for later multi-product launches.

## Technical fluency, hands-on

Specifies and builds. Closes the distance between deciding what to do and having a
working thing.

- Architected the **LLM feature evaluation framework** across 2K test subjects,
  cutting critical AI failures **27%** and lifting response consistency **45%**.
- Shipped an operations CRM, a predictive financial and risk dashboard, and an iOS
  application — designed, built and deployed independently.
- Built ten client web platforms end to end, including this site, using AI-assisted
  development.

## Quality governance

Raises the standard rather than meeting it, and catches the thing that would have
shipped.

- Set and enforced a **≥95% production validation bar** across the Streamer 4K
  launch.
- Deprecated a misaligned setup-quality KPI and institutionalized Overall Setup
  Success as the single source of truth across all hardware lines.
- Caught a late-stage voice remote usability defect and turned it into gated user
  feedback checkpoints across every downstream program in the portfolio.
