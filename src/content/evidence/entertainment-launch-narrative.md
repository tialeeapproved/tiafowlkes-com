## The problem

Gate reviews decide whether a product ships. The people in the room need bug
data translated into a decision, not a bug list — and the translation is where
launches get delayed or shipped wrongly.

## What I did

I owned the narrative into each gate review: which risks were real, which were
noise, what the mitigation path was, and what I recommended. Go or no-go, with
the reasoning attached, in front of the GM.

## Outcome

Twenty launch markets, and a 4.5 satisfaction rating across the portfolio.
