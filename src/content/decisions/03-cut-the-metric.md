headline: Cut the metric leadership was reporting on.
context: Setup quality across the Chromecast line was gated on a first-try setup measure that no two teams defined the same way. Launch readiness reviews stalled on what the number meant instead of whether the product was ready.
call: Remove first-try setup from readiness reviews and launch criteria, and establish overall setup success as the primary KPI.
followed: Product, Android TV, and software leads aligned on a single measure. Readiness reviews moved from arguing definitions to acting on results, and the change held across subsequent launches.
